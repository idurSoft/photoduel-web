1. What data we process

Account: email address or the identifier of your sign-in via Apple or Google, plus technical sign-in data.

Device: when you create a new account, an identifier of your device – stored only as an irreversible hash value, not the identifier itself – and, on the iPhone, two flags that Apple stores for our app for your device via DeviceCheck. We use this to count how many accounts were created on a device.

Profile details: first name, username (if chosen), date of birth, comparison group (gender), height and – only if you provide it voluntarily – your ethnic group.

Photos: the photos you upload and their order in your profile.

Usage: your decisions in duels, the values calculated from them (e.g. Elo, rank, win rate), the time of your last activity, as well as your appearances in other people's duels.

Feedback: messages you send us via "Report a bug or share an idea", together with the app version, operating system and device model.

2. Purposes and legal bases

We process your data to provide the app: to run your account, show your photos in duels and evaluate ratings (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

To protect against abuse, such as automated or manipulated ratings or accounts created in bulk, and for the security of the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest). This includes that at most three new accounts can be created on one device within twelve months.

Providing your ethnic group is voluntary. We only process it with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR) and only to check, in aggregated anonymous analyses, whether ratings are fair for all groups. It affects neither duels nor leaderboards and is never visible to others. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting the information in Settings.

3. Automatic review of your photos

Every uploaded photo is checked automatically before it appears in duels: whether exactly one face is visible, whether it contains prohibited content and whether the person shown is obviously a minor. Age and gender are also estimated in order to assign the photo to the right comparison group. In a second step, we check whether it is a real photo (e.g. not a screenshot). People are not identified; no face profiles are stored.

The review serves to protect minors and other users and to ensure fair assignment (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). Before your first upload, we also explicitly ask for your consent to your photos being transferred to the services named below for this purpose (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). If a photo is rejected, you can upload another one or contact us.

4. Who sees your photos

What "anonymous" means for us: neither your name nor your username (the latter only if you allow it in Settings) appears next to your photo, and nobody can search for your name to find out your score. Ratings are anonymous: nobody learns who rated which photo and how.

Your photo itself, however, is visible to other users: it appears in other people's duels and afterwards in their duel history. It only appears on the leaderboard and in the comparison if you turn that on in Settings under "Visibility" (default: off), and even then only while it is in the upper half of its comparison group – for users of your own comparison group as well as for users viewing other groups. Anyone who taps it there sees it enlarged together with Elo score, percentile, rank and number of duels. If the setting is off or your photo is in the lower half, it only counts there as an anonymous placeholder, without picture or name. Only you see your own score in full. Anyone who recognizes you in a photo shown this way can attribute these values to you. So only upload photos for which you are fine with this. You can turn visibility off again at any time and pause, remove or delete a photo at any time (see Terms, section 3).

You can report other people's photos and block people. For this we store who reported what or blocked whom. A reported photo is hidden for you immediately; after reports from several devices we temporarily remove it from the app; if the person who owns the photo appeals, we review it personally. You and blocked people no longer see each other's photos (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

5. Service providers

We work with carefully selected service providers. They process data only on our behalf.

Hosting, database and sign-in service: storage of your account, your photos and ratings. The data is located in a data center in the EU (Ireland).

Photo storage service (provider based in the USA): storage of your photos and their smaller versions. The photos are stored in data centers in the EU.

A cloud provider's image recognition service: automatic review of photos (face, prohibited content, age and gender estimation).

An external provider's AI service (USA): automatic check of whether it is a real photo.

Email delivery service (USA): internal notification to us when a photo has been reported several times.

Apple Push Notification Service: delivery of notifications if you have allowed them. For this we store a device token with your account; it is removed when you sign out. You can turn off notifications at any time in the iOS settings.

Apple and Google: if you sign in through them. Their privacy policies also apply.

Apple DeviceCheck: on the iPhone, to count the accounts per device (see section 1). For this Apple only stores two flags for your device, without reference to your account.

Data processing agreements are in place with the service providers. Where data is transferred to the USA, this is done on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU standard contractual clauses. On request, we will tell you the specific recipients.

6. Storage period

We store your data as long as your account exists. If you remove a photo, it stays stored with its values under "Past photos" until you delete it there for good; then we delete the image, its values and its history. If you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), we take your photos out of the app immediately and keep your account for another 14 days so that you can restore it by signing in. After that we delete your photos and your profile details and block the sign-in. Ratings you gave are kept without reference to you so that other users' results stay correct.

If you delete your account, we only keep an irreversible hash value of your Apple or Google sign-in. Its sole purpose is to let you create an account again with the same sign-in without it counting toward the per-device limit. The count of accounts per device expires twelve months after the first counted account.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. To do so, write to us at the email address below.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the authority where you live.

8. Minimum age

Photocel is for ages 18 and up. We check this using the date of birth.

9. This website

This website is provided via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc. (USA). When you open a page, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the page and protect it against abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The transfer to the USA is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More in GitHub's privacy statement: docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

The website sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and uses no analytics or tracking services.
