1. Scope

These terms apply to the use of the Photocel app. The provider is Rudi Umar (see Legal Notice).

2. Account and minimum age

To use the app you need an account and must be at least 18 years old. Your details must be true. One account per person is allowed.

You create new accounts via Apple or Google. At most three new accounts can be created on one device within twelve months.

3. Your photos

You may only upload photos that show yourself and for which you hold the necessary rights. In particular, photos of other people, nudity, violence and offensive or unlawful content are prohibited.

You grant us the simple right, limited to the lifetime of your account, to display your photos in the app so that other users can rate them. We may remove photos that break these rules.

Visibility: the app is anonymous in the sense that no name appears next to your photo and nobody can search for you. However, your photo is visible to other users – in duels, in the duel history, on the leaderboard and in the comparison, in your comparison group as well as for users viewing other groups. Anyone who taps it sees it enlarged with Elo score, percentile, rank and number of duels. Anyone who recognizes you can attribute these values to you. By uploading, you agree to this.

Zero tolerance applies to objectionable content and abusive behavior. Every photo is checked automatically before its first duel. You can report other people's photos and block people at any time. We review reported content within 24 hours; we remove violating photos and ban the accounts of those responsible.

Review on upload: if a photo is not approved in the automatic review, we show you the reason in the app (for example no face visible or several people in the picture). You can then upload another photo.

Blocking: if you block a person, you no longer see each other's photos – neither in duels nor on the leaderboard, in the comparison or in the duel history. The person is not informed. You can lift a block at any time under Settings → Blocked people.

Pausing: a paused photo no longer appears in new duels, but stays visible with its values on the leaderboard, in the comparison and in other people's duel history.

Removing: a removed or replaced photo moves to "Past photos". There you continue to see its values and can reactivate it as long as a slot is free. It is no longer visible to others – neither in duels nor on the leaderboard or in the comparison. In other people's duel history the duel and its result remain, but the photo itself is no longer shown there.

Deleting for good: if you delete a photo in "Past photos", the image, its Elo score, its votes and its history are deleted irrevocably. Duels already decided remain anonymously for the values of the other photos, without the image.

Example photos during the launch phase: as long as there are still few real users, example photos are mixed in with the real photos – in duels, on the leaderboard and in the comparison. They do not show Photocel users (they are licensed stock photos), and ratings always come from real users only. For every new real photo we take out one example photo of the same age and gender group, until only real photos are left.

Reported photos: if a photo is reported several times, we may temporarily remove it from the app – faster in the case of serious allegations such as nudity, violence or hate or suspicion of a minor. The photo then moves to your "Past photos"; you are informed in the app and – if you have allowed notifications – by notification. You can see it there but not reactivate it. If you think the block is wrong, you can appeal there; we then review the photo personally. If it is fine, you can reactivate it afterwards; if it breaks these rules, it stays blocked.

4. Fair use

Rate honestly. Automated, coordinated or obviously random ratings may be treated as invalid; in the case of serious or repeated violations we may ban your account.

We may limit the number of ratings per period. When the limit is reached, the arena pauses; the app shows you when it continues. You can upload at most 50 photos per day.

You can only vote on one device at a time. If you are signed in on several devices, you can choose in the app on which one you continue.

Content of the app such as photos, ratings, Elo scores, leaderboards and statistics may only be viewed in the app and only for your own personal use. In particular, automated extraction (e.g. by scripts, bots or scrapers), mass saving or copying, and any passing on, publication or use for other services or purposes are prohibited. The content and its compilation are legally protected. We may limit how much content can be retrieved per period and, in the event of violations, restrict access or ban the account.

5. Free use

The app is free. For every duel you decide, one of your photos gets an appearance in other users' duels. There is no entitlement to a certain number of appearances within a certain time.

8. Notifications

If you allow it, we send notifications to your device when something important happens with your photos or your score, for example when a photo has been approved. Notifications are voluntary; you can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

9. Termination

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. Your photos then disappear from the app immediately. For 14 days you can restore the account by signing in again – with photos and values. After that it is deleted for good: your photos (the image files) are deleted and the sign-in is blocked. Duels already decided remain anonymously so that the values of the other photos stay correct.
