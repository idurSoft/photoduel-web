Support

Answers to the most common questions. And how to reach us if something doesn't work.

CONTACT

Write to us right from the app.

Under Settings → Report a bug or share an idea. We'll get back to you as soon as possible.

A support email address will be added before the app launches.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I delete my account?

In the app under Settings → Delete account. Your photos disappear from the app immediately. For 14 days you can restore the account by signing in again; after that it is deleted for good.

How do I report a photo or block a person?

Tap the flag on the photo. There you can block the person or report the photo with a reason. We review reported photos within 24 hours.

My photo wasn't approved. Why?

Every photo is checked automatically before its first duel. The app shows you the reason, for example when no face or several people can be seen. You can then upload another photo.

What is the minimum age for Photocel?

18. We check this using your date of birth.

How accurate is my score?

Like in a study: the more duels a photo has, the more accurate its score.

Who sees my photos?

Other users see your approved photos in duels, on the leaderboard and in the comparison, also enlarged and with their score. We only show your name if you have made it public.

Who votes on my photos?

Real people, anonymously. Your score is compared with people of your gender and a similar age.

How many photos can I upload?

Up to six. Every photo gets its own score, so you see which one works best.

Does Photocel cost anything?

No, Photocel is free.

Why can't I continue right now?

To prevent abuse, there are limits on how much you can vote and browse per day. The app shows you when you can continue.

Can I vote on several phones?

You can only vote on one phone at a time. On another phone, just tap "Continue here".
